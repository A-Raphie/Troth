Market Friction

The $2.4 Trillion Commercial Trust Dilemma

Digital labor and machine services move at internet speed, but the financial layer holding them together is predatory, slow, and fractured.

Web2 Freelance Rakes
Platforms like Upwork and Fiverr extract 10% to 20% rakes, freeze contractor earnings in mandatory 5-to-14 day clearing holds, and resolve disputes through opaque, centralized customer service desks that default against independent workers.
20% Platform Cut 14-Day Clearing Hold
Direct Crypto Risk
Sending raw crypto transfers forces 100% counterparty risk onto one party. Either the buyer pays upfront and risks contractor abandonment, or the worker delivers upfront and risks non-payment. There is no middle ground.
Zero Escrow Guarantee Counterparty Ghosting
Legacy Chain Gas Floor
Ethereum L1 and legacy rollups require users to hold volatile native tokens (ETH) and incur $1 to $15 transaction fees. This makes micro-milestones ($10–$50 tasks) and machine-to-machine micropayments completely unviable.
Volatile Gas Token Micropayments Impossible

Step 02
Claimable Links
